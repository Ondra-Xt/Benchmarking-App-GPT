# TECE compatibility diagnostics

TECE source-pack compatibility reporting is diagnostic-only. The report generated by
`tools/report_tece_compatibility_diagnostics.py` groups extracted source-pack rows by
catalogue page-range label, TECE family, role candidate, nominal length, and article
number, then proposes body/channel/drain to cover/grate/plate pairings for manual audit.

The report never promotes TECE rows into canonical `Products`, `Comparison`,
`BOM_Options`, `Final_Assemblies`, or `Final_Set_Details`. Every report row and family
summary keeps `production_promotion_blocked=true`, `ready_for_benchmark=false`, and
`ready_for_customer_view=false`.

## Evidence levels

Compatibility pairings are tagged with one of these diagnostic evidence levels:

- `explicit_article_level_matrix` — a source row contains explicit matrix/table evidence
  tying the body/drain article to the cover/grate article. This is high-confidence
  evidence, but TECE still remains blocked until the wider production gate has complete
  technical evidence and policy approval.
- `explicit_text_pairing` — source text explicitly states that one article is compatible
  with, suitable for, paired with, or otherwise assigned to the counterpart article. This
  is also high-confidence evidence.
- `explicit_section_pairing` — articles are in the same TECE family catalogue section or
  page range. This is diagnostic/proposal-only.
- `same_length_same_family_candidate` — articles share nominal length and family. This is
  diagnostic/proposal-only and is **not** production compatibility evidence.
- `insufficient_evidence` — no reliable pairing evidence is available.

Only `explicit_article_level_matrix` and `explicit_text_pairing` can set the diagnostic
`explicit_article_level_compatibility_evidence_exists` flag. They still do not make TECE
production-ready by themselves.

## Family notes

- TECEdrainline diagnostics highlight candidate drain bodies around `650000`-`650004`,
  cover/grate/plate tables in the `600xxx`/`601xxx`/`650xxx` ranges where extractable,
  and preserve conditional `10 mm Aufstau` / `20 mm Aufstau` flow values from the
  source-pack loader.
- TECEdrainprofile diagnostics identify profile and plate candidates around
  `670xxx`/`671xxx` and drain bodies `673001`, `673002`, and `673003`.
- TECEdrainway diagnostics group body, set, and accessory candidates, but do not promote
  assemblies without explicit article-level assembly evidence.
- TECEdrainpoint S diagnostics identify point-drain body and cover candidates only for
  manual review.

## Future production promotion requirements

Future TECE production promotion requires approved TECE source evidence with explicit
article-level compatibility/assembly evidence, complete article-level technical data,
manual review of conditional values, and an explicit production-gate change. Same
nominal length, same family, or same catalogue section alone must never be used as a
production compatibility rule.
